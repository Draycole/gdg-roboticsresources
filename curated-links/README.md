## Entry format

| Resource | Type | Level | Why it is here | Last checked |
|---|---|---|---|---|

- **Type:** book, course, video series, documentation, repo, tool
- **Level:** beginner, intermediate, advanced
- **Why it is here:** one sentence, including who should skip it
- **Last checked:** month and year you confirmed the link works and the content is current

## Rules

- No entry without a reason.
- Prefer maintained, licensed, beginner-friendly material.
- Re-check links at the start of each cohort and mark dead ones.
- Link, do not copy, unless the license allows it and the file is small and essential. If you copy, keep the license and credit the source.

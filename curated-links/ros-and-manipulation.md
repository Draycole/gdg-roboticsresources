# ROS and manipulation (stage 5)

> Seed list written from general knowledge. Verify each link and fill in "Last checked" before merging.

## Start here, in this order

1. **ROS 2 documentation, beginner tutorials**: [docs.ros.org](https://docs.ros.org). Pick the distribution that matches your Ubuntu version and do the command-line and client-library tutorials in order.
2. **Modern Robotics** (Lynch and Park), book and video lectures. Read the chapters on forward and inverse kinematics once you can describe a two-link arm.
3. When our stage 5 labs exist, do the Arduino-to-ROS 2 bridge before anything advanced.
